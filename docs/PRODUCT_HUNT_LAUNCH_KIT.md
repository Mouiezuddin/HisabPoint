## 3. 💬 Maker's First Comment (The Launch Story)

> **Important**: Post this comment immediately (within 60 seconds) after the launch goes live. This sets the tone for community discussion.

```markdown
👋 Namaste Product Hunt! 

I'm thrilled to introduce **HisabPoint (हिसाब पॉइंट)** — a modern, tactile FinTech ledger engine engineered to bridge grassroots retail culture with high-performance web engineering.

### 📖 The Origin Story
If you've ever stepped inside any neighborhood Kirana store, spice dealer, or general merchant across India or South Asia, you’ve seen it: a thick, red cloth-bound register with brass corners known as **Bahi Khata (बही खाता)**.

Over **30 million small merchants** manage billions in customer credit inside these paper books. But physical registers carry heartbreaking real-world problems:
- ❌ **Zero backups**: Prone to monsoon rain damage, tea spills, fire, or theft.
- ❌ **Math errors & balance drift**: Merchants spend hours recalculating running balances by hand, losing hard-earned money to arithmetic mistakes.
- ❌ **Awkward debt collection**: Without digital proofs, chasing dues creates friction and damaged relationships.

Existing accounting software is either built for corporate accountants (too many complex debit/credit forms) or clunky and slow.

### 💡 What We Built
We wanted to preserve the **sacred, tactile trust of traditional Bahi Khata** while giving merchants the speed, safety, and intelligence of modern cloud computing:

1. 📔 **Tactile Skeuomorphic Aesthetic**: A warm parchment-and-leather UI designed to feel natural to non-tech-savvy merchants while looking stunning on retina screens.
2. 🛡️ **Zero Balance Drift Engine**: Balances are *mathematically derived* from atomic transaction entries in real time. We strictly prohibit storing static balance columns, eliminating desynchronization.
3. ⚡ **Rapid-Fire Transactions**: Record credit extended (`+ Given`) or repayments (`- Received`) in under 3 seconds.
4. 🧾 **Itemized POS Invoicing & GST**: Generate tax bills, apply discounts, and atomically sync credit sales directly to customer ledgers.
5. 💬 **WhatsApp Statements & Print Bills**: Send clean debt summaries directly into customer WhatsApp chats with a single click, or print thermal/A4 invoices.
6. 📱 **Offline-Ready Lightweight PWA**: Complete installable experience under 1.2 MB total footprint that runs on everything from a $50 Android phone to a MacBook Pro.
7. 🔐 **Enterprise Security**: TOTP Two-Factor Authentication, login lockout protection, and row-level tenant isolation.

### 🧪 Test Drive It Live (No Signup Needed!)
We’ve pre-loaded a full interactive demo store so you can explore the engine immediately:

### 🙏 We'd Love Your Feedback!
- How does the skeuomorphic notebook aesthetic feel compared to conventional flat SaaS designs?
- What features would help small business merchants in your region the most?
- We're currently planning **Voice-to-Ledger Multilingual AI** (speak in Hindi/Urdu/English to log entries) and **UPI automated reconciliation** — what would you prioritize next?

Thank you for your support, and happy hunting! ❤️
```

> Key engineering decisions:
> 1. **Derived Balance vs Static Columns**: Instead of maintaining a mutable `current_balance` on customer models that drifts whenever concurrent writes fail, customer balances are dynamically derived from atomic immutable transaction rows.
> 2. **Tenant Isolation**: Row-level isolation on every query via `request.user.id` and DRF custom permissions.
